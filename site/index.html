What it can actually do

Not a chat box pretending to be an assistant. Aegis drives the computer you are already looking at — the same apps, the same files, the same screen.

👀

Sees your screen

Captures your display and reads it with a vision model — so “what does this error mean?” is a real question.

⌨️

Controls your PC

Runs commands, opens apps, types, presses shortcuts, clicks. It can do the thing, not just describe it.

📂

Works with your files

Lists, reads, writes and searches files — while refusing credential folders like .ssh and .aws.

🛡

Asks before acting

Safe mode is on by default: a command runs only after you press Approve on a card showing exactly what it will do.

🔍

Finds the model you already have

Reads Ollama, LM Studio and GGUF folders directly — even when nothing is running — and starts the server for you.

🗣

Says “Hey Aegis”

Say the phrase instead of clicking the mic. Off until you switch it on, and the phrase is matched on your own machine — but the transcription comes from your browser’s speech engine.

🔄

Updates itself

Checks GitHub, verifies the SHA-256 before unpacking, and keeps the previous build as Aegis.old.

Download

One zip per OS, built by CI from this repository and published with a SHA-256 you can check. Already have an older Aegis? It offers this build by itself.

Getting a brain takes one press

Aegis is the assistant; the model is what it thinks with. If you already have one downloaded, it will find it.

Install the app and open it. If you have no model yet, it says so plainly instead of failing.

Open ⚙️ Settings → 💻 This computer. It shows your CPU, memory and graphics card and what fits.

Press 🔍 Find the model I downloaded. It reads Ollama, LM Studio and GGUF folders — even with nothing running — starts the server, and picks the best model it found.

Or download one in the panel. It recommends a model that fits your RAM and downloads it, with a progress bar you can cancel.

Privacy, without the marketing

“Private” is easy to claim and hard to mean. Here is the actual boundary.

What stays on your PC

  • With a local model: your screen, your files, your clipboard, every prompt
  • Your access token, stored owner-only and never readable by the agent
  • Your settings and conversation history
  • The whole app works with the network unplugged

What you should know

  • A cloud provider (if you choose one) sees the screenshots and files you ask about
  • Safe mode asks — it does not sandbox. Approved commands run with your privileges
  • A local model is not a filter; the safety rules are code, not prompts
  • Hosting it on your LAN exposes a remote-control endpoint; the token is required, but only use networks you trust
  • The wake word is matched locally, but it is transcribed by your browser — in Chrome and Edge that engine is Google’s, so audio can go to them

The full threat model is in SECURITY.md — including what is not protected.
